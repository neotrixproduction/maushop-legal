MauShop

Delete your MauShop data

MauShop has no accounts, passwords or emails. Each install gets a random anonymous ID. Data reaches our server only if you allowed "Share usage data".

Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open MauShop and go to the Me tab.
  2. Tap Privacy & data.
  3. Tap Delete my data on the server.

Everything tied to your anonymous ID is deleted right away: activity, sessions, simulated orders, MauPay history, interest summary, device info and the anonymous ID itself. Sharing is switched off.

Can't open the app any more?

Email aldygital.business@gmail.com with the subject "MauShop data deletion". If you can, include your anonymous ID (Me › Privacy & data › Your anonymous ID). We delete the data within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is not on our server: your cart, wishlist, orders and MauPay wallets live on your phone. Uninstalling MauShop or using "Reset app" in the Me tab removes them.

Hapus data MauShop

MauShop tidak memakai akun, kata sandi, atau email. Setiap instalasi mendapat ID anonim acak. Data hanya sampai ke server kami jika kamu mengizinkan "Bagikan data pemakaian".

Hapus di aplikasi (langsung)

  1. Buka MauShop, masuk ke tab Saya.
  2. Ketuk Privasi & data.
  3. Ketuk Hapus data saya di server.

Semua data untuk ID anonimmu langsung dihapus, termasuk ID-nya, dan berbagi data dimatikan.

Tidak bisa membuka aplikasi?

Kirim email ke aldygital.business@gmail.com dengan judul "Hapus data MauShop", sertakan ID anonimmu jika ada. Kami hapus dalam 30 hari dan membalas setelah selesai.
