MauShop

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

EnglishBahasa Indonesia

Short version: MauShop is a pretend shop. Nothing is sold, shipped or charged. We collect usage data only if you tap "Allow", only under an anonymous ID, and you can turn it off or delete it any time.

Who we are

MauShop ("the app") is an entertainment app that simulates an online marketplace. Products, stores, prices, couriers and the MauPay balance are all made up. MauPay is play money: it is free, it cannot be bought with real money and it cannot be cashed out.

What stays on your phone

Your cart, wishlist, orders, MauPay wallets, country choice and settings are stored on your device only. We never receive them unless you allow usage data (see below). "Reset app" in the Me tab erases them.

Usage data (only if you allow it)

After you start using the app we ask once: "Help make MauShop better for you?". If you tap "No thanks", nothing is sent. If you tap "Allow", the app sends the following to our server, tied to a random anonymous ID created for your install:

DataExamples
App activityscreens opened, products and stores viewed and for how long, searches, cart and wishlist changes, simulated orders, MauPay top-ups and spending, check-ins
Ads in the appwhich in-app promotions were shown or tapped (these are MauShop's own pretend ads; no ad network is used)
Sessionswhen the app was opened and closed, how many screens
Device and settingsplatform (Android or web), app version, chosen country, language, time zone, screen size, browser user agent, approximate memory and CPU cores
Interest summaryproduct categories you seem to like, worked out from the activity above

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any payment details. We do not ask for any phone permissions.

Why we use it

To understand which parts of the app people enjoy, fix problems, and improve product recommendations inside the app. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to show ads outside the app.

Where it is stored

Usage data is stored with Supabase (our database provider) and sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Only the MauShop team can read it.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it, or at most 24 months after your last use of the app, whichever comes first.

Your choices

  • Turn it off: Me › Privacy & data › switch off "Share usage data". Nothing more is sent.
  • Delete it: Me › Privacy & data › "Delete my data on the server". This removes everything we hold about your anonymous ID, at once. See also how to delete your data.
  • Uninstall: removes all data on your phone.

Product photos

Some product photos load from a public image service (DummyJSON). That service sees a normal web request from your device (such as your IP address), as with any website image.

Children

MauShop is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: aldygital.business@gmail.com

Kebijakan Privasi

Terakhir diperbarui: 25 November 2026

Singkatnya: MauShop adalah toko pura-pura. Tidak ada barang yang dijual, dikirim, atau ditagih. Kami hanya mengumpulkan data pemakaian jika kamu menekan "Boleh", hanya dengan ID anonim, dan kamu bisa mematikan atau menghapusnya kapan saja.

Tentang kami

MauShop ("aplikasi") adalah aplikasi hiburan yang meniru marketplace online. Produk, toko, harga, kurir, dan saldo MauPay semuanya rekaan. MauPay adalah uang mainan: gratis, tidak bisa dibeli dengan uang asli, dan tidak bisa dicairkan.

Yang tetap di HP kamu

Keranjang, wishlist, pesanan, dompet MauPay, pilihan negara, dan pengaturan hanya disimpan di perangkatmu. Kami tidak menerimanya kecuali kamu mengizinkan data pemakaian. "Reset app" di tab Saya menghapus semuanya.

Data pemakaian (hanya jika kamu izinkan)

Setelah kamu mulai memakai aplikasi, kami bertanya sekali. Jika kamu menekan "Jangan", tidak ada yang dikirim. Jika kamu menekan "Boleh", aplikasi mengirim ke server kami, dengan ID anonim acak untuk instalasimu: aktivitas di aplikasi (layar, produk dan toko yang dilihat serta lamanya, pencarian, keranjang, wishlist, pesanan simulasi, top up dan belanja MauPay, check-in), promo di dalam aplikasi yang tampil atau diketuk, sesi pemakaian, info perangkat (platform, versi aplikasi, negara pilihan, bahasa, zona waktu, ukuran layar, user agent, perkiraan memori dan jumlah inti CPU), serta ringkasan kategori yang kamu sukai.

Kami tidak mengumpulkan nama, email, nomor HP, kontak, foto, lokasi tepat, atau data pembayaran apa pun.

Untuk apa

Untuk memahami bagian aplikasi yang disukai, memperbaiki masalah, dan membuat rekomendasi produk di dalam aplikasi lebih pas. Kami tidak menjual datamu dan tidak membagikannya ke pengiklan.

Penyimpanan dan lama simpan

Data disimpan di Supabase (penyedia database kami) dan dikirim lewat koneksi terenkripsi (HTTPS). Data disimpan sampai kamu menghapusnya, atau paling lama 24 bulan setelah terakhir kamu memakai aplikasi.

Pilihanmu

  • Matikan: Saya › Privasi & data › matikan "Bagikan data pemakaian".
  • Hapus: Saya › Privasi & data › "Hapus data saya di server". Semua data untuk ID anonimmu langsung dihapus. Lihat juga cara menghapus data.
